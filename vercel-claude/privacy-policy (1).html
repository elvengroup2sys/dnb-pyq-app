Privacy Policy

Effective date: 23 June 2026  ·  Published by Elven Studios

This Privacy Policy explains what information DNB Medicine PYQ ("the App", "we", "us") collects when you use our website or mobile application, and how that information is used and protected.

1. Information We Collect

We collect only what is needed to run your account and unlock the content you've paid for.

  • Account information. When you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address, and profile photo through Firebase Authentication.
  • Subscription status. We store whether your account has unlocked premium access (true/false) and the related transaction reference, in our Firestore database.
  • Payment details. On the web app, payments are processed by Razorpay. We never see or store your card, UPI, or bank details — Razorpay handles this directly and shares only a payment success confirmation with us. On the Android app, payments are processed by Google Play Billing, which is subject to Google's own privacy practices.
  • Usage data. If Google Analytics is enabled on the site, we may receive anonymised, aggregate usage statistics (such as pages viewed and session length). This data is not tied back to your identity by us.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To let you sign in and keep your premium access linked to your account.
  • To verify and record successful payments.
  • To respond to support requests.
  • To understand overall usage and improve the app (only if analytics is enabled).

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties for marketing purposes.

3. Third-Party Services

We rely on the following processors to operate the app. Each has its own privacy policy governing the data they handle on our behalf:

  • Google Firebase — authentication and database hosting
  • Razorpay — payment processing (web)
  • Google Play Billing — payment processing (Android, where applicable)
  • Google Analytics — anonymised usage statistics, if enabled

4. Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored in Google Firestore. Access is restricted by Firestore security rules so that only your own authenticated account can read or write your data. Payment credentials and API secrets are stored server-side in encrypted environment variables and are never exposed to the browser.

5. Data Retention

We retain your account and subscription data for as long as your account is active, so that your premium access remains available across devices. You may request deletion at any time (see Section 7).

6. Children's Privacy

DNB Medicine PYQ is designed for postgraduate medical students preparing for the DNB examination and is not directed at, or intended for use by, children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18.

7. Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data by contacting us at the email below. We will respond within a reasonable time.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected with a new effective date at the top of this page.

9. Contact Us

For any privacy questions or data requests, write to: dnbpyq@gmail.com
