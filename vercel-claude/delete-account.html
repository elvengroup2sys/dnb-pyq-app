Delete Your Account & Data

This page explains how you can request deletion of your DNB Medicine PYQ account and the personal data associated with it. We respect your right to control your data and will action verified requests promptly.

How to request deletion

DNB Medicine PYQ does not yet offer an in-app delete button. To request deletion of your account and associated data, contact us by email from the address linked to your account:

Send your request to
dnbpyq@gmail.com
1
Email dnbpyq@gmail.com from the email address you use to sign in to the app.
2
Use the subject line “Delete my account” so we can identify your request quickly.
3
We will verify the request is from the account owner and confirm once your account and data have been deleted.

Requests are typically completed within 7 days and no later than 30 days from receipt.

What data is deleted

When your deletion request is processed, we permanently remove the following data associated with your account:

  • Deleted Your account login (email address and authentication record)
  • Deleted Your unique user identifier
  • Deleted Your access / purchase status record (including premium access flag and order reference)

What data may be retained

For legal, accounting, and fraud-prevention reasons, a limited set of records may be retained for a period after account deletion:

  • Retained Payment and transaction records held by our payment processor (Razorpay) and required for tax and accounting compliance. These are retained for the period required by applicable law (typically up to 8 years under Indian financial regulations) and are not used to identify you within the app after deletion.

We do not store your card or banking details ourselves; card payments are handled directly by Razorpay.

Questions

If you have any questions about deleting your account or how we handle your data, email us at dnbpyq@gmail.com or review our Privacy Policy.
